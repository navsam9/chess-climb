Privacy Policy

Chess Climb: Tactics Trainer

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Chess Climb does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your puzzle history, rating, theme skills, review schedule and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple includes this data in your backup according to your backup settings.

What we collect

Nothing. Chess Climb has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. The app does not send any data over the internet.

Links to Lichess

If you tap Lichess on a puzzle, that puzzle opens on lichess.org in your browser. The Lichess privacy policy applies there.

Exporting your history

You can export your puzzle history as a CSV file from Settings. The file goes only where you choose to send it.

Deleting your data

In Settings, tap Reset all progress, or delete the app from your iPhone.

Children

Chess Climb collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
