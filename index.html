Chess Climb

Tactics Trainer for iPhone

Chess Climb is an endless feed of chess puzzles that adapts to you. Solve a puzzle, see how you did, and the next one appears. The app learns which themes you miss and brings you similar puzzles until you get them.

Questions

How is my rating calculated?

Chess Climb uses Glicko-2, the same rating system Lichess uses for puzzles. Solving a puzzle raises your rating and missing one lowers it. How much depends on how hard the puzzle is. Your first 15 puzzles find your level, so your rating moves quickly at first.

Does solving speed affect my rating?

Only while the app is finding your level. After that, your rating counts only solves and misses. Your speed still helps the app decide how hard your next puzzles should be.

Why did a hint lower my rating?

Using a hint counts as a miss. The Hint button asks you to tap twice, so you can't use one by accident.

Can I skip a puzzle?

No. If you're stuck, tap Give up to see the solution. It counts as a miss.

Can I go back to an earlier puzzle?

Yes. Tap Back to see earlier puzzles and step through their solutions. You can retry them without affecting your rating.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All puzzles are stored on your iPhone, and everything works offline.

How do I start over?

In Settings, tap Reset all progress. This deletes your rating, history and review schedule.

Where do the puzzles come from?

From the Lichess open puzzle database, which anyone may use freely (CC0). Chess Climb is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lichess.
