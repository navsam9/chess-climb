More puzzles in the app

Chess Climb is an endless feed of chess puzzles that adapts to you. Today's climb is in it too.

  • Puzzles at your level. Each one is matched to your rating and aimed at the themes you most need to practice.
  • Misses come back. Miss a puzzle and a similar one comes a few puzzles later. The one you missed returns after 12 hours.
  • A rating for every theme. Forks, pins, back-rank mates, endgames and dozens more, each explained in plain English.
  • An engine to check your ideas. After a puzzle, see the best line or try your own moves.
  • Free, with no ads and no account. A million puzzles on your phone, so it works offline.
