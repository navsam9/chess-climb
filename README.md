# Chess Climb site

The Chess Climb website, published with GitHub Pages: Today's climb to play in the browser, and the support, privacy and get-the-app pages for the iPhone and Android apps.

- Today's climb: `index.html`, with `assets/` (the page's script and rules, its styles, the pieces under their BSD license, the app's sounds) and `climbs/`, one file a month of each day's ten puzzles. The app repository's `Tools/export_climbs.sh` writes them with the apps' own code, so the site and both phones give the same ten; it is run again after a catalog rebuild and before the last month runs out. Nothing is sent from the page: the day's progress stays in the browser.
- Opening the app from the site (apps 1.3.1 and later): `.well-known/apple-app-site-association` and `.well-known/assetlinks.json` let the iPhone and Android apps claim the climb page, `/` and nothing else, so the link in a shared score opens the app on Today's climb's card; support, privacy and get stay in the browser. On an iPhone, Safari's banner (`apple-itunes-app` in `index.html`) offers Open to a visitor who has the app. On Android, `manifest.webmanifest` names the app, so Chrome can tell a visitor who has it, and the page's Get the app buttons become Open the app.
- Support: `support/index.html`
- Privacy policy: `privacy.html`
- Get the app: `get/index.html`, the one link for bios. iPhones go straight to the App Store, except inside app browsers such as TikTok's, which block it: there the page asks to be opened in Safari. Android shows Google's Get it on Google Play badge, and inside app browsers a note to open the page in Chrome.
- Search engines and AI assistants: `robots.txt` lets every crawler read the whole site and points to `sitemap.xml`, which lists the four pages. `index.html` carries structured data (JSON-LD) describing the site and the app, and its app section and `noscript` note say in plain text what a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript would otherwise miss. `googlef9da424c3f1f9447.html` keeps the site verified in Google Search Console: don't remove it.
