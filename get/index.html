Android

Chess Climb for Android is in testing on Google Play. Getting it takes three steps.

Open this page in Chrome first: use this app's menu (⋮ or ⋯) to open it in your browser. Signing in to Google often fails inside other apps.

  1. Join the testers group, using the Google account on your phone. Join the group
  2. Become a tester, then come back to this page. Become a tester
  3. Install from Google Play with this button. Searching the store won't find Chess Climb while it's in testing. Install from Google Play

If step 2 says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again. Please keep the app for at least 14 days: Google needs 12 testers for two weeks before it can go public.
